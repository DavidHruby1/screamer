What is the price?

Nothing. Screamer is MIT-licensed. You pay your speech provider, or nothing on a local server.

Does history keep my recordings?

Never audio. History is opt-in and keeps up to 100 text entries, encrypted with DPAPI.

What if I switch windows mid-dictation?

The text is held back. Press Ctrl+Alt+Shift+V in the right app, or open History.

Is it Windows only?

Yes. It relies on Windows keyboard hooks, SendInput and DPAPI.
